# License

## Replicant GEN 1

### Open Hardware (CERN-OHL-W v2.0)

Replicant GEN 1 design files (including the airframe and the **battery rail/mount** that forms part of the drone) are released under the **CERN Open Hardware Licence v2 — Weakly Reciprocal (CERN-OHL-W-2.0)**.

That means you may **download, manufacture, modify, and sell** drones based on these files. If you **modify the licensed design files** and then **distribute those modified files** or **ship products based on your modified licensed design**, you must make the **corresponding modified design source** for the licensed portions available under **CERN-OHL-W-2.0**, and keep the required copyright/licensing notices.

:::info What's allowed
- ✅ Download and use the design files
- ✅ Manufacture drones for personal use
- ✅ Manufacture and sell drones commercially
- ✅ Modify the designs for your own needs
- ✅ Distribute modified designs (under CERN-OHL-W-2.0)
:::

### Proprietary / Excluded (Battery Pack)

The **rail-mounted battery pack** itself is **not** part of the open release. Battery pack design (cell configuration, wiring, electrical design, drawings, and any files explicitly marked "Proprietary") remains proprietary to **Yirra Systems** and is **not licensed** for copying, manufacturing, or redistribution without written permission.

:::warning Excluded from open license
- ❌ Battery pack design
- ❌ Battery pack cell configuration
- ❌ Battery pack wiring
- ❌ Any files marked "Proprietary"
:::

## Three ways to license (read once)

One question, three doors. **Free** under CERN-OHL-W-2.0 for makers, research, and teaching. **Partner (CPA)** for commercial up to 2,000 units / yr. **Enterprise (MCLA)** for anything above 2,000, exclusivity, or defence and government programs. No retroactive penalties — CERN breach is a copyright problem with a 30-day cure, not a surprise invoice. Use the [three-doors page](/docs/compliance) to pick, or the [compliance guide](/docs/compliance-guide) to document conformance on the Free door.

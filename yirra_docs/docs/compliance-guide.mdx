# CERN-OHL-W-2.0 Compliance Guide (v1.0)

**Official PDF (v1.0):** [Download Yirra Replicant GEN1 Compliance Guide (PDF)](/legal/Yirra_Replicant_GEN1_Compliance_Guide_v1_0.pdf) · [Open in new tab](/legal/Yirra_Replicant_GEN1_Compliance_Guide_v1_0.pdf) — same document as summarised on this page.

This guide summarises **what you must do to stay on the free CERN-OHL-W-2.0 path** for Replicant GEN 1 CAD and related materials published on this site. It is a practical overview — it does not replace the [license text](https://ohwr.org/cern_ohl_w_v2.txt) or legal advice.

## Who should read this

- Anyone **downloading** design files from [CAD Downloads](/docs/downloads)
- Anyone **modifying** CAD and **distributing** those modifications or **shipping products** based on modified licensed design
- Anyone mixing open files with **proprietary add-ons** in a distribution

If you cannot meet these obligations, the **commercial fallback** on the [License](/docs/license) page applies — review the fee table there and contact [business@yirrasystems.com](mailto:business@yirrasystems.com) early.

## Core obligations (summary)

1. **Preserve notices** — Keep copyright, licence, and documentation notices that ship with the files.
2. **Source for modifications** — If you distribute modified CAD or products based on modified licensed design, make the corresponding source available under CERN-OHL-W-2.0 on the same terms.
3. **Attribution** — Credit Yirra Systems / Replicant GEN 1 as the upstream when you redistribute.
4. **Licence notice** — Make it clear recipients get the same licence (CERN-OHL-W-2.0) for the licensed portions.
5. **No extra legal restrictions** — Do not impose terms that contradict the open licence on those materials.
6. **Document changes** — Clearly describe what you changed and where to find your source.

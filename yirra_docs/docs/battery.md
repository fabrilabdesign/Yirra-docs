# Battery System

A modular, rail-mounted battery system built for rapid swaps and professional performance. The battery's mass and rigidity enhance flight stability and damp vibrations for cleaner footage.

<BatteryGallery />

---

## Construction & Materials

- **EVE 50 PL Cells** → High discharge rates, long cycle life
- **Pure Nickel Tabs** → Minimal resistance, maximum power delivery
- **10AWG Wiring** → Reduced voltage drop and heat
- **CF Nylon Case** → Impact resistance and structural integrity
- **Concealed Balance Connector** → Protected from disconnection and contamination

## Why It Matters

The battery isn't just a power source — it's a structural component. Its mass damps vibrations for cleaner footage. The rigid construction stabilizes flight dynamics. And the rail system lets you swap batteries in seconds, not minutes.

**This is not open source.** The battery pack uses spot-welded EVE cells and proprietary power management. We don't release these files for safety — improper cell handling is a fire and injury risk.

# Free and Open — Replicant GEN 1

**For makers, tinkerers, researchers, and students.**

## Who this is for

You want to build, learn, research, or teach with the Replicant GEN 1 platform. You are **not** running a commercial manufacturing business with it.

## What you can do

- Build one for yourself, or for your class, lab, or club.
- Fork the design. Change anything you like.
- Share your changes publicly.
- Sell small numbers at cost or for fundraising, as long as you follow the licence.

## What it costs

**Zero dollars.** The design files are free under CERN-OHL-W-2.0.

**Example:** Print a frame, solder the flight controller, fly it in your backyard. No invoice, no account, no reporting.

Batteries from Yirra are non-partner-priced at <strong>AUD ${OPEN_BATTERY_PRICE_AUD} / unit</strong>. Partner pricing kicks in at the [Partner door](/docs/compliance/partner).

## What you promise (four things)

1. **Publish your changes.** If you modify the design and share the hardware with others, you share the design files too.
2. **Credit** Yirra Systems and the Replicant GEN 1 project.
3. **Don't imply** Yirra endorses you or your fork.
4. **Don't attack other users with patents.** If you sue someone over this design, your own licence ends.

## What happens if you mess up

The licence **ends automatically**. You lose the right to build or share the design until you fix it. We don't send lawyers. You just lose the licence. CERN-OHL-W-2.0 section 7 tells you how to cure within 30 days and restore your rights.

## Get the files

Download the design files at [yirrasystems.com/download](https://yirrasystems.com/download). No login, no form, no fee.

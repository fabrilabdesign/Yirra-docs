# Yirra Partner — Commercial up to 2,000 units

**For workshops, local manufacturers, and small brands.**

## Who this is for

You are building Replicants to sell. You're under **2,000 units a year**, you want a clear price, and you want to be listed as a Yirra Systems Commercial Partner.

Above 2,000 units / yr? → [Yirra Enterprise (MCLA)](/docs/compliance/enterprise).

## What you can do

- Manufacture and sell Replicants commercially, at your chosen tier.
- Get listed on [yirrasystems.com](https://yirrasystems.com) as a Yirra Partner.
- Buy genuine batteries and key parts at partner prices.
- Use the "Yirra Partner" badge on your product and site.

## What it costs — pick a tier

<PartnerTierCards />

Prices are **AUD, ex-GST**. Setup is paid once a year. Report and royalty are due within **14 days of each quarter-end**.

### Worked examples

- **Maker, 20 units:** $1,100 setup + 20 × $20 royalty = **$1,500** + batteries at $175
- **Commercial, 200 units:** $4,500 setup + 200 × $15 royalty = **$7,500** + batteries at $149
- **Scale, 1,000 units:** $13,500 setup + 1,000 × $9 royalty = **$22,500** + batteries at $125

## What you promise (five things)

1. **Report** your unit count within 14 days of every quarter-end. Use the portal.
2. **Pay** royalties within 14 days of submitting that report.
3. **Build** with genuine batteries and flight controllers from the approved list.
4. **Keep records** of what you manufactured and sold, so we can reconcile your reports.
5. **Don't export** to parties blocked under Australian or applicable law.

## What happens if you mess up

We talk to you first. If a report is late or a royalty is missed, you get **30 days to fix it**. If you keep missing, we pause your licence, invoice the backpay, and remove you from the partner list. No surprise lawsuits.

## Apply

Pick a tier and buy directly. The Partner Agreement is emailed for e-signature within **1 business day**. Your Good Standing Certificate is issued on countersignature.

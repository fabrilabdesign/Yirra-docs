# Pick a tier. Pay. Ship.

The **Partner door** is the commercial path for workshops, local manufacturers, and small brands. One agreement, published tiers, partner battery pricing, and a **Good Standing Certificate** you can show procurement or customs. Three tiers covering up to **2,000 units / yr**. Above that, use [Yirra Enterprise (MCLA)](/docs/compliance/enterprise).

Checkout is live on [yirrasystems.com](https://yirrasystems.com).

## Why this door exists

CERN-OHL-W-2.0 is free, but it comes with four standing promises and a full publish-your-changes requirement every time you distribute. That's fine for makers and research — the [Free door](/docs/compliance/free) is for you.

If you're **selling** Replicants, the Partner door is the cheaper path once you count:

- **Partner battery pricing** — save $54 / $80 / $104 per unit vs the $229 non-partner price
- **No publish-your-changes obligation** on your proprietary firmware or customisations
- **Listed on yirrasystems.com** with the *Yirra Partner* badge
- **Design-file updates** as we ship new revisions

And, unlike the v1.0 framing, **there is no retroactive "non-compliance licence"**. CERN breach is a copyright problem with a 30-day cure, not a sudden invoice. See the [three doors comparison](/docs/compliance).

---

## What's included at every tier

- **Commercial licence** to manufacture and sell products built on Replicant GEN 1
- **Good Standing Certificate** — signed annually, valid for procurement, customs, and customer assurance
- **Partner listing** on yirrasystems.com + the *Yirra Partner* badge
- **Partner battery pricing** at tier rate (save $54 / $80 / $104 per unit)
- **Design file updates** as we ship new revisions
- **Email support** with tier-appropriate review SLA

Unit cap: **2,000 units / yr** (Scale ceiling). Above that, use [Enterprise](/docs/compliance/enterprise).

---

## How payment works

1. Click **Buy on yirrasystems.com** on the tier above — you're taken to the live Stripe-backed product page.
2. Complete checkout with a card or invoice (**AUD, ex-GST**).
3. We email the Partner Agreement for e-signature within **1 business day**.
4. On countersignature your Good Standing Certificate and partner dashboard access land in your inbox.
5. Report unit counts within **14 days of each quarter-end**. Pay royalties within **14 days of the report**.

Annual fee renews automatically unless cancelled 30 days before anniversary.
